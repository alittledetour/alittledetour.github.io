What exactly is a detour?

A Little Detour started as a running note of things we kept getting sidetracked by — a drawer system, a lamp, a fragrance we couldn't stop thinking about. Eventually the note turned into a habit, and the habit turned into this.

We write about two things: homes that work harder in small spaces, and beauty routines that stay organised without becoming a chore. Everything here has actually been tried, used, and lived with — not pulled from a trending list.

A small note on honesty: some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a little something if you buy through them. It costs you nothing extra, and it's never the reason something made the list.
